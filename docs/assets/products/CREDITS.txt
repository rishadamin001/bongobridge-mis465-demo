BongoBridge illustrative photography — MIS 465 educational demo

These are real reference photos illustrating fictional catalog items and generated
maker scenarios. They are not photos of stock offered for sale. Brand labels visible
in a photograph do not identify the fictional seller or imply an endorsement.
Export-maker photos illustrate product types; they are not verified images of
Bangladeshi workshops or actual artisan goods. No user image is uploaded anywhere.

Runtime image files are bundled with the site. They do not contact image providers.
Files were resized and compressed for this website; CSS may crop the display.
